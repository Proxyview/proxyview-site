Why now

Evidence stopped being proof.

Three changes ended the assumption that a record of something is proof it happened.

One

Fabrication became free

A convincing record of an event that never occurred now takes seconds and costs nothing.

Two

Machines began deciding

Automated systems release funds and settle claims faster than any review can follow.

Three

Machines began reporting

Drones, robotics and agents now produce their own account of the world for other systems to act on.

The failure nobody catches

Checking whether a photograph was edited is not the same as checking whether it shows what it claims to show. A real, unedited photograph — of a different site, a different year, or someone else's damaged property — passes every integrity check available today, because the image itself is completely genuine. The picture is true. The claim attached to it is not. That is the failure that costs institutions money, and nothing on the market is built to catch it.

What authentication looks like

Every submission produces a signature — a shape formed by how well its independent characteristics agree with one another and with the wider record of that place and time.

An authentic event produces a full, regular form: everything corroborates everything else. A fabricated or misattributed one cannot hold that shape. Something fails to agree, and the form collapses.

Why this is a machine-learning problem

Authenticating a claim about the physical world means reasoning across noisy, partially missing signals against an adversary constructing evidence to look genuine — large-scale multimodal modelling, adversarial robustness, probability calibration, and a reference corpus maintained at global scale.
